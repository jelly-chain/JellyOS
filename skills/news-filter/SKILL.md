# News Filter

Filter and score news for trading impact with real-time alerts.

## Capabilities

- **Impact Scoring**: Rate news by market impact
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Bullish/bearish classification
- **Event Detection**: Regulatory, macro, protocol events
- **Multi-source**: RSS feeds, APIs, social
