# Gas Sniper

Find optimal gas prices and schedule transactions during low-fee periods.

## Capabilities

- **Price Prediction**: Predict gas price movements
- **Transaction Scheduling**: Delay tx until optimal fees
- **Chain Comparison**: Compare across EVM chains
- **MEV Protection**: Suggest times for MEV avoidance
