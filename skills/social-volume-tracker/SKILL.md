# Social Volume Tracker

Detect abnormal social media activity before price moves.

## Capabilities

- **Volume Spike Detection**: Abnormal mention frequency
- **Sentiment Tracking**: Bullish/bearish ratio over time
- **Influencer Monitoring**: Key account activity alerts
- **Cross-Platform**: Twitter, Reddit, Telegram, Discord
- **Momentum Scoring**: Social momentum leading indicators

## Commands

- `/social volume <symbol>` — volume analysis
- `/social sentiment <symbol>` — sentiment score
- `/social influencers <symbol>` — key accounts talking
- `/social alerts` — active social alerts
