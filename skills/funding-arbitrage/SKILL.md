# Funding Arbitrage

Trade funding rate differentials across perpetual exchanges.

## Capabilities

- **Rate Comparison**: Binance, Bybit, OKEx, dYdX
- **Arbitrage Signals**: Long on low-funding, short on high-funding
- **Historical Tracking**: Rate history and trends
- **Risk Assessment**: Basis changes and volatility
