# News Catalyst

Trade market-moving news with AI-powered impact scoring.

## Capabilities

- **Real-time News Feed**: Aggregated from 20+ sources
- **Impact Scoring**: AI-rated 0-100 impact score
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Bullish/bearish classification
- **Affected Assets**: Auto-detect which tokens are impacted
- **Historical Backtest**: How similar news moved markets

## Commands

- `/news scan` — latest market-moving news
- `/news impact <headline>` — score a news item
- `/news affected <headline>` — which assets are impacted
- `/news history <type>` — historical news impact
