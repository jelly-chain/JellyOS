# Cross-Chain Bridge Monitor

Track capital flows between chains for rotation signals.

## Capabilities

- **Bridge Volume**: Daily volume per bridge protocol
- **Net Flow**: Inflow minus outflow per chain
- **Capital Rotation**: Detect chain-to-chain capital movement
- **Bridge Security**: TVL and security status per bridge
- **Cost Analysis**: Bridge fees and timing comparison
