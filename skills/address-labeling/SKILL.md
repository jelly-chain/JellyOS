# Address Labeling

Identify and label known blockchain addresses.

## Capabilities

- **CEX Detection**: Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, OKEx hot wallets
- **DEX Router**: Uniswap, SushiSwap, Curve routers
- **Protocol Contracts**: Aave, Compound, Maker, Lido
- **Whale Database**: Top 1000 richest addresses
- **ENS Resolution**: Reverse ENS lookup
