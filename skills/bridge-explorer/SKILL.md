# Bridge Explorer

Find optimal cross-chain routes with fee estimation and security scoring.

## Capabilities

- **Route Finding**: Best bridges for asset/chain pairs
- **Fee Calculation**: Gas + bridge fees
- **Security Scoring**: Bridge risk assessment
- **Timing**: Estimated arrival times
