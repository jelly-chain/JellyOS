# Staking Dashboard

Compare staking yields across chains and validators.

## Capabilities

- **Validator Comparison**: Uptime, commission, MEV
- **Yield Optimization**: Best chain/validator combos
- **Slashing Risk**: Historical slashing events
- **Compounding**: Auto-compound calculations
- **Unbonding**: Lock-up periods and liquidity

## Commands

- `/stake chains` — supported staking chains
- `/stake validators <chain>` — validator list
- `/stake compare <asset>` — yield comparison
- `/stake compound <position>` — compound rewards
