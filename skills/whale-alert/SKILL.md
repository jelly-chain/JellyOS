# Whale Alert

Monitor large transactions in real-time.

## Capabilities

- **Size Thresholds**: Configurable entry/exit amounts
- **Chain Monitoring**: Multi-chain whale tracking
- **Trend Analysis**: Accumulation vs distribution
- **Address Tracking**: Follow specific whale addresses
- **Alert Webhooks**: Slack, Discord, Telegram notifications
