# Rug Pull Predictor

Identify tokens at high risk of rug pulls before they happen.

## Capabilities

- **Ownership Analysis**: Check if ownership is renounced or concentrated
- **Liquidity Lock**: Verify LP token locks and unlock dates
- **Contract Risk**: Honeypot, mint, freeze, blacklist functions
- **Dev Wallet Tracking**: Monitor developer wallet activity
- **Social Verification**: Team doxxing and social presence checks
