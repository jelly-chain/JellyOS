# Rekt Stacks

Check if tokens or addresses have been involved in known exploits or rug pulls.

## Capabilities

- **Rekt Database**: Query known exploit transactions
- **Risk Scoring**: Aggregate risk scores for tokens
- **Exploit History**: Past exploits by token/address
- **Verification**: Check against CertiK and other audits
- **Alert Integration**: Get notified on risky interactions
