# Pairs Trader

Trade statistical relationships between correlated assets.

## Capabilities

- **Cointegration Testing**: Johansen test for long-term relationship
- **Z-Score Monitoring**: Entry/exit thresholds
- **Correlation Analysis**: Dynamic correlation tracking
- **Half-Life Calculation**: Mean reversion timing
- **Stationarity Check**: ADF test for equilibrium
