# Synthetic Asset Watch

Track synthetic assets for depeg events and arbitrage opportunities.

## Capabilities

- **Price Deviation**: Synthetic vs underlying price gap
- **Depeg Detection**: Early warning for peg breaks
- **Arbitrage Signals**: Buy/sell opportunities on depeg
- **Collateral Ratio**: Monitor collateralization levels
- **Peg Stability**: Historical peg stability scoring
