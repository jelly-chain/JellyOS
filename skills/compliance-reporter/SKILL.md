# Compliance Reporter

Generate reports for tax and audit purposes.

## Capabilities

- **Tax Reports**: Capital gains, income, staking rewards
- **Audit Trails**: Full transaction history export
- **Regulatory Export**: FATCA, FBAR ready formats
- **Portfolio Snapshots**: Historical portfolio values
- **Trade Reconciliation**: Match trades to exchanges
