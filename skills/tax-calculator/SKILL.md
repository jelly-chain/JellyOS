# Tax Calculator

Calculate crypto taxes with cost basis tracking, gain/loss reporting, and jurisdiction-specific rules.

## Capabilities

- **Cost Basis**: FIFO/LIFO tracking
- **Gain/Loss**: Realized and unrealized calculations
- **Reporting**: CSV export for tax forms
- **Jurisdiction**: Support for US, EU, UK, Asia
